EAST — Free Sample
A few sprites from every pack of EAST, a Japanese-inspired pixel art collection, so you can drop them into a
scene together and see how they fit. Everything is drawn at one scale (14 m = 96 px small, 192 px large), from
one light, with a shadow as a separate image for every sprite.

  bamboo/   a bamboo stand and a sakura through the four seasons with their wind loops, a maple in autumn, irises (54 files)
            full pack: https://mmorwynn.itch.io/bamboo-pack
  houses/   a machiya town house in all four looks, a thatched minka, rice bales, a lantern stand (40 files)
            full pack: https://mmorwynn.itch.io/east-houses-pack
  gardens/  a kasuga lantern in all four looks, a koi pond, the red bridge, and the bamboo fence: all 16 autotile pieces (88 files)
            full pack: https://mmorwynn.itch.io/gardens-pack
  shrines/  a vermilion torii in all four looks, the fox guardians, a worship hall, a pagoda (28 files)
            full pack: https://mmorwynn.itch.io/shrines-pack

Each folder keeps the layout of its full pack, so code written against the sample works with the full pack.
The full packs: https://mmorwynn.itch.io
